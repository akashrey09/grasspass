# Golden Hour Chase
*Grass Pass #001 · Afternoon · post-monsoon · 45 min*

**Back by 17:34** (sunrise 06:31, sunset 18:17)

Low sun makes everything look different. Spend the best light of the day on purpose.

## Steps
- [ ] **13 min.** Walk with the sun at your side, never looking straight at it, and find three things lit gold.
- [ ] **9 min.** Find the longest shadow you can and pace out its length.
- [ ] **9 min.** Turn around and notice the colours on everything lit from behind.
- [ ] **14 min.** Sit somewhere with a wide view and watch the light change until time is up.

## Notice
- Shadows stretching across the ground
- Colours you only see in low light
- Which way birds and insects are moving
- Greening after the rains, migrant birds arriving, still warm at midday.

## Bring
- Water

## Afterwards, on paper
- What was the best-lit thing you saw?
- How long was your longest shadow in paces?
